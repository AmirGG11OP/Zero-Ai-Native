## Overview
This document serves as the technical data dictionary for the `FinalLog.csv` dataset generated by the Zero Cancer Reactor. The CSV file contains the output log of a continuous execution spanning over 72,000 simulation epochs (ticks). 

Running on a CUDA C++ engine (tested on an RTX 3060 12GB GPU), the simulation processes the mathematical interactions of up to 70 million cellular agents. The system utilizes a Proportional-Integral-Derivative (PID) controller (`BiologicalPID`) and models Lotka-Volterra dynamics to establish a regulated host-tumor equilibrium. Through these mechanisms, the engine logs long-term simulated temporal data, tracking parameters such as exosome absorption rates (`TotalExosomesAbsorbed`) and systemic inflammation values.

## Usage / How to Run
For forensic analysis, machine learning ingestion, and verification of the 72,000 recorded epochs of simulated host-tumor interaction, the complete dataset can be downloaded here: [FinalLog.csv](FinalLog.csv).

### Granular Telemetry Lexicon: The 69 Variables

The following outlines the variables recorded in the CSV, directly correlating to the C++ structures defined in `ReactorEngine.h` (`BioTelemetry`).

#### Temporal & Population Dynamics
*   **`EpochTick`:** The compute cycle counter representing one complete update of the cellular matrix.
*   **`TotalPopulation`:** The absolute number of live agents within VRAM, tracked via atomic counters (`d_current_population_`).
*   **`HealthyCells`:** Count of agents operating without senescence or oncogenic markers.
*   **`SenescentCells`:** Agents that have reached programmed telomere limits (`telomere_length <= CRITICAL_TELOMERE_LENGTH`) but have not undergone apoptosis.
*   **`ApoptoticCells`:** Agents undergoing cell death logic during the recorded tick.
*   **`OncogenicCells`:** Count of cells marked with `FLAG_Z_TUMOR_MARKER`, featuring modified telomerase logic.

#### Cellular Health & Genomic Integrity
*   **`AvgTelomere`:** The mean telomere base-pair length across all agents. Monitored to evaluate stabilization parameters.
*   **`AvgMutationLoad`:** Average modeled genomic instability, influenced by `ROS_Storm` and balanced against `dna_repair_efficacy`.
*   **`AvgAge`:** Mean chronological lifespan of the population (in epochs).
*   **`MortalityRate`:** Ratio of apoptotic/necrotic cells to `TotalPopulation` per tick.
*   **`TissueToxicity`:** Accumulation of modeled necrotic waste, utilized as a parameter to influence `SystemicInflammation`.
*   **`SystemicInflammation`:** A derived parameter calculated from the balance of pro-inflammatory (`IL-6`, `TNFa`) and anti-inflammatory (`IL-10`) values.
*   **`ResourceDrain`:** Modeled metabolic load, calculated via glucose/oxygen consumption heuristics.

#### Control Theory & Exosome Network
*   **`ImmortalityControl`:** An output index (0.0 to 1.0) correlated to the stability of the tumor saturation parameters.
*   **`TumorProbability`:** The statistical threshold determining spontaneous cell state changes based on `AvgMutationLoad`.
*   **`ActiveExosomes`:** Programmatic count of signaling vesicles circulating in the simulation environment.
*   **`TotalExosomesAbsorbed`:** A cumulative counter tracking the number of times cells intercept an exosome parameter, triggering state resets.
*   **`PID_Signal` / `PID_P` / `PID_I` / `PID_D`:** Real-time variables of the `BiologicalPID` loop designed to regulate `ZTumorSaturation`.

#### Vital Organ & Endocrine Axis
*   **`AvgExhaustion`:** Localized ATP/metabolic depletion values assigned to agents.
*   **`HepaticStress`:** Modeled liver toxicity load. Designed to interact with simulated Cori Cycle pathways for lactate conversion.
*   **`RenalGFR`:** Modeled Glomerular Filtration Rate representing metabolite clearance.
*   **`EndocrineSOS`:** A binary/scaled systemic distress signal evaluating combined hepatic/renal stress.
*   **`HyperHepatic` & `HyperRenal`:** Scalar multipliers simulating organ functional scaling.
*   **`BrainGlucose` / `CardiacPerfusion` / `PulmonaryO2`:** Modeled vital capacity parameters.
*   **`Adrenaline` / `Cortisol` / `Dopamine` / `Testosterone` / `Estrogen`:** Endocrine variables mapped to modulate immune responses, tissue integrity, and cardiac scaling.

#### Cytokine Network
*   **`ZTumorSaturation`:** The ratio of oncogenic cells to carrying capacity. Target set by the PID controller.
*   **`IL2`:** Modeled growth factor. Increases during simulated tumor pruning to prompt CD8+ expansion.
*   **`IL4` & `IL13`:** Modeled tissue repair signals associated with M2 macrophage polarization.
*   **`IL6`:** Modeled pro-inflammatory cytokine parameter.
*   **`IL10` & `IL35`:** Immunosuppressive signals utilized to regulate aggressive immune loop behaviors.
*   **`IL12`:** Inflammatory signal modeling M1 macrophage activation.
*   **`IFNg` (IFN-Gamma):** Cytokine secreted by simulated CD8+ and NK cells. Correlated with the upregulation of PD-L1 values.
*   **`TNFa`:** Modeled tumor necrosis factor, capable of inducing widespread apoptosis logic.
*   **`TGFb`:** Immunosuppressive parameter used to simulate stromal shielding and recruit Tregs.
*   **`CXCL8` & `CCL2`:** Chemokine parameters directing localized immune agent deployment.
*   **`G_CSF` & `M_CSF`:** Variables dictating the simulated production rate of new leukocytes.

#### WBC Lineages
*   **`Neutrophils`:** Modeled cellular agents responding to raw damage parameters.
*   **`M1` & `M2`:** Polarization states of modeled macrophages (M1 for target removal, M2 for tissue matrix repair).
*   **`NK`:** Natural Killer agents targeting cells with specific MHC-I logic downregulation.
*   **`CD8`:** Cytotoxic T-Cells modeled to induce targeted apoptosis.
*   **`CD4`:** Helper T-Cells serving as a multiplier for CD8+ efficacy via IL-2 mapping.
*   **`BCells`:** Modeled antibody producers tracking exosome saturation.
*   **`Tregs`:** Regulatory T-Cells modulated by TGF-b to suppress CD8+ activity and prevent absolute tumor clearance.
*   **`Tcf1_CD8`:** A simulated memory reservoir ensuring continued evolutionary pressure responses.

#### Effector Mechanisms & Checkpoint Matrix
*   **`PerfGranzyme`:** Effector pathway utilized by CD8+ cells to induce apoptosis, evaluated via mathematical formulas in the `BiologicalTickKernel`.
*   **`FasL`:** Receptor-mediated apoptosis protocol.
*   **`MAC`:** Membrane Attack Complex. Countered programmatically by the cellular `cd59_shield` value.
*   **`ROS_Storm`:** Reactive Oxygen Species stress parameter, capable of inducing local cell instability.
*   **`TissueRegen`:** Wnt/VEGF pathways used to recalculate matrix integrity post-pruning.
*   **`PDL1_Axis`:** Evasion parameter calculated via Michaelis-Menten kinetics to blind CD8+ execution logic.
*   **`CTLA4`:** Secondary immune checkpoint variable modulating T-Cell activation thresholds.
*   **`TregAura`:** Localized immunosuppressive scalar generated by Treg populations.
*   **`TIM3` & `LAG3`:** Variables representing T-Cell exhaustion. High expression mathematically dampens lymphocyte efficacy, contributing to system equilibrium.

## Assumptions & Limitations
1.  **Temporal Equivalence Assumption:** Claims interpreting "72,000 epochs" as equivalent to "1,300 biological years" rely entirely on arbitrary programmed mappings of simulation ticks to real-world time. This is a functional assumption of the engine, not a validated chronological fact.
2.  **Mathematical Biology vs. Empirical Data:** The variables representing cytokines, WBC lineages, and effector mechanisms (e.g., `IL2`, `PerfGranzyme`) are governed by mathematical abstractions (e.g., Gaussian noise, Sigmoid curves) designed to mimic homeostasis. They do not directly reflect empirical, in-vivo biochemical kinetics.
3.  **Variable Clamping:** The output value of "0.0% Systemic Inflammation" during specific phases is the result of explicit programmatic constraints (e.g., `std::clamp` and conditional logic) designed to force the system into a stable state.
4.  **Simulated Exosomes:** The `TotalExosomesAbsorbed` counter represents millions/billions of programmatic variable updates within the agent logic, not the physical generation and tracking of individual biological vesicles.
